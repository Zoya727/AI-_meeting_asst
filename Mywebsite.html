Privacy and trust

What happens to your notes

Offline by default

In offline mode, notes are processed in your browser. Saved meetings stay in this browser's local storage, and nothing is uploaded.

AI mode is your choice

If you switch to AI mode, the notes go to your chosen Anthropic or Google Gemini API with your own key. The key is never saved.

Human review first

Exports unlock only after you confirm you checked the minutes against the source notes.

Compliance: this is a project prototype. It holds no SOC 2 or GDPR certification and has no accounts, servers or audit logs, so it should not be used for confidential or regulated recordings. Those controls would be required before real client use.
